=== L3 ===

OEPS_PN_Datamatrix: Serial number without spaces
OEPS_PN: Serial number in the format [1]
MPN: Manufacturer part number
OEPS_Lot_QR: Lot in the format [2]
Lot_MMYY: Lot's MMYY
Lot_Packing: Lot's Packaging (four chars)
Lot_Random: Lot's Random (four chars)
Quantity: Quantity (if decimal, use always a dot '.')
OEPS_Quantity_Datamatrix: Quantity (append a '0' before the quantity) (if decimal, use always a dot '.')


[1]
If 10 chars: XXXX XX XXXX
If 11 chars: XXXX XXX XXXX

[2]
MMYY_PACK_RAND

PACK is mapped in this way:
Reel > REEL
Tube > TUBE
Tape > TAPE
Bag > _BAG
Box > _BOX
Tray > TRAY
Spool > Spool
Other > OTHR

RAND are four random letters (upper or lower case) or numbers.
