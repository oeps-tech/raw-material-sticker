OEPS_PN_barcode: Serial number without spaces
OEPS_PN_text: Serial number in the format [1]

=== L3 ===

Datamatrix: <version>*<serial_number>*<lot>*<quantity>*<checksum>
OEPS_PN: Serial number in the format [1]
MPN: Manufacturer part number
Lot_Packaging: The package as is (Other, Bag, Tray, ...)
Lot: The entire lot in the format [2]
Quantity: Quantity (if decimal, use always a dot '.') without extra zeros, i.e., remove any zeros at left and right, except "0.123".

<version> - 1
<quantity> - The minimum quantity length is 6 digits and the maximum is 10, add zeros before if needed to complete this request. Always use a '.' as separation if need to use decimal numbers.

[1]
If 10 chars:
	If: Y is a letter: XXXX YXX XXX
	If: Y is a number: XXXX YX XXXX
If 11 chars: XXXX XX XXXXX

[2]
MMYY-PACK-RAND

PACK is mapped in this way:
Reel > REEL
Tube > TUBE
Tape > TAPE
Bag > BAG
Box > BOX
Tray > TRAY
Spool > SPOL
Other > OTHR

RAND are four random letters (upper case only) or numbers.
